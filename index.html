ESP32 Screen

A small touchscreen by my front door, also called Entrance Display.

It is a personal hobby project: a 2.8-inch ESP32 display that shows the time, the local weather, the S&P 500 and the temperature indoors. It connects to my own Google Nest thermostat so the screen can show the indoor temperature and switch the heating between Heat, Eco and Off or change the target temperature.

It is not a public service. Nobody else can sign up for it or use it, and it has no website beyond these two pages.
